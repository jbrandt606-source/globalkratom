Cardiac Toxicity & Arrhythmia Risk

Clinical reports and toxicologic literature documenting arrhythmias, conduction abnormalities, cardiac arrest, and severe cardiovascular events associated with kratom and mitragynine exposure.

2026 researchArrhythmiasCardiac arrestMechanismsSystemic toxicityEvidence limits

Key findings

  • Human reports describe ventricular fibrillation, Brugada-pattern ECG changes, QT prolongation, cardiac arrest and ventricular dysfunction following kratom exposure.
  • Reported outcomes include defibrillation, intensive care and permanent cardiac devices.
  • Experimental ion-channel findings provide plausible mechanisms, but do not establish population risk.
  • Concomitant exposures, electrolyte abnormalities and underlying susceptibility must be considered.
