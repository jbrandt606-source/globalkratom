Cardiac Toxicity & Arrhythmia Risk

Published medical reports and laboratory research examining abnormal heart rhythms, disrupted electrical signals, cardiac arrest and other serious heart complications associated with kratom and mitragynine exposure.

2026 researchArrhythmiasCardiac arrestHeart electrical activityOrgan damageResearch findings

Key findings

  • Published patient reports describe life-threatening abnormal heart rhythms, changes in the heart’s electrical activity, cardiac arrest and weakened heart pumping after kratom exposure.
  • Some patients required electrical shocks to restore heart rhythm, intensive care, permanent pacemakers or implanted defibrillators.
  • Laboratory studies suggest that kratom can interfere with the electrical signals that regulate the heartbeat. These studies do not establish how frequently heart complications occur in people.
  • Other substances, abnormal blood mineral levels and pre-existing susceptibility can also affect heart rhythm.

Dunz et al. — Sudden cardiac arrest following kratom tea consumption (September 23, 2026)A young man went into cardiac arrest after drinking kratom tea. Blood testing found 500 ng/mL of mitragynine, a major kratom alkaloid. Tests found no structural or genetic heart disorder. Kratom Cardiotoxicity – Reversible Brugada Pattern & QTc ProlongationThe patient developed major abnormalities in the electrical activity of the heart. The QTc, a measure of the time the heart takes to electrically reset, initially reached 654 milliseconds and later measured 552 milliseconds. Electrical conduction was also slowed (QRS 160 milliseconds). The abnormalities improved after stopping kratom. Testing of the product detected kratom alkaloids but no other drugs; mild blood mineral imbalances were also present.Archived PDF
