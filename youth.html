Youth Exposure & Adolescent Risk

Evidence related to pediatric exposure, adolescent use, retail accessibility, poison‑center reports, and youth‑oriented normalization of kratom products.

New researchPoison center dataClinical evidenceRetail productsSocial media

Evidence updated November 27, 2026

Key Findings

  • Poison centers recorded 707 exposures in the original youth age-group table from 2019–2024, including 459 in the youngest group.
  • A 2026 study of children under 12 found that almost all ingestions were accidental; the median age was two years.
  • Published pediatric reports document serious breathing problems, abnormal heart rhythms and hospital care.
  • Newborns exposed during pregnancy have required medication to treat opioid withdrawal.
  • Retail packaging and online promotion are relevant to understanding how children may encounter these products.
