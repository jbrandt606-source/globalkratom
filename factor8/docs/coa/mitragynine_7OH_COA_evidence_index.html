Preserving and using the record

PRESERVATION AND AUTHENTICATION
Preserve the complete original report and its hosting page, not just a potency crop.
Record the public URL and retrieval date, and calculate a file hash after downloading.
Keep the original file unchanged; annotate a separate working copy. Respect report
instructions concerning full reproduction and seek permission for excerpts if needed.
Match the lab sample ID, client-provided lot, package photograph and product identity.
Confirm whether a listed lot is the manufacturer's lot or the submitter's identifier.
Ask for original issue/revision dates and resolution of inconsistencies.
Request assay-specific accreditation scope, reference-standard identification,
chromatographic separation/selectivity, reporting limits and measurement uncertainty.
For contested identification, ask the laboratory what confirmatory testing is available.
A chromatographic detector named in a report is not, by itself, proof of poor quality.

UNITS AND NONDETECTIONS
ND means not detected at the applicable limit, not proven absent.
<LOQ means below the quantification limit; do not replace it with zero.
Not listed/not tested is not ND.
1% by mass equals 10 mg/g, but percentage of total alkaloids is a different denominator.
Use the laboratory's specific unit and serving definitions; mg/unit can refer to an
entire submitted multi-serving package. “Total alkaloids” means the reported panel,
not every chemically possible compound. Do not combine 7-OH and MP without labeling
the sum. A solvent result cannot establish a specific synthesis route. A Pass label
does not establish efficacy, comprehensive safety, or lawful marketing.

REQUEST TO THE ISSUING LABORATORY
“I am compiling a regulatory evidence record. Please confirm whether your laboratory
issued report [ID], provide the complete current authorized version, and identify any
revisions. Please confirm the submitted sample and lot identifiers, whether product
identity was independently verified or supplied by the client, the units and sample
mass underlying per-unit values, assay-specific accreditation scope, and the method's
ability to distinguish mitragynine, 7-hydroxymitragynine, pseudoindoxyl and relevant
interferents. Please also state whether the report may be reproduced in full in a
public regulatory submission.”

WHAT WOULD CONNECT THESE REPORTS TO MANUFACTURE
Seek authenticated supplier and manufacturing records that link an incoming
mitragynine lot to an outgoing 7-OH lot, with corresponding COAs and a knowledgeable
manufacturer or chemist's statement. None of the cross-vendor comparisons in this
index supplies that connection. Do not infer a principal manufacturing route from
concentration alone.

LEGAL REFERENCE (SEPARATE FROM THE COA FINDINGS)
21 U.S.C. 802(23) addresses principal-compound use, the immediate chemical-intermediary
relationship, and necessity of control. These are not findings a commercial COA makes.
