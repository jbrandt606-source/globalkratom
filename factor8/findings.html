What the evidence supports, what remains uncertain, and what DEA is being asked to determine.

Factor Eight / Part VIII: The Findings Requested

Prong One: Substantial support

The 7-OH preparative chemistry, patents and commercial accounts support a serious principal-compound argument. Direct commercial input records would provide additional corroboration.

Prong Two: Strongest for 7-OH

The direct 7-OH chemistry is stronger than the presently documented MP pathway. Evaluate MP separately, including its alternative syntheses.

Prong Three: The critical determination

The necessity argument must connect the requested control to a prospective restriction in manufacture. Public-health harms alone do not satisfy this requirement.

Requested agency action

Evaluate mitragynine under 21 U.S.C. §§ 802(23) and 811(e), with a clearly specified controlled target, proposed regulatory text and scope. Alternative ordinary scheduling relief can be presented separately.

Source distinction: patents, company statements, scientific findings and litigation allegations are not interchangeable. The proposed DEA findings have not been made by the agency.
