Part II: The Chemistry

From mitragynine to 7-hydroxymitragynine and pseudoindoxyl: separate pathways, separate claims.

Factor Eight / Part II: The Chemistry

The 7-OH pathway

Preparative studies and patent examples describe production of 7-hydroxymitragynine from mitragynine. These laboratory demonstrations are stronger evidence of a manufacturing relationship than human metabolism alone.

The MP pathway

Some routes proceed through 7-OH; a Shaman Pharma patent also discloses an alternative pathway involving mitragynine and MP. The identity of each compound and the status of each route must be checked against the source.

The alternative route

The Angyal total-synthesis study reports an MP preparation that does not rely on mitragynine. It does not automatically defeat a precursor argument, but any claim of exclusive dependence would be unsupported.

Source distinction: patents, company statements, scientific findings and litigation allegations are not interchangeable. The proposed DEA findings have not been made by the agency.
