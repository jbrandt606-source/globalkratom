Part III: The Patent Record

What preparative patents demonstrate—and what they cannot establish about commercial adoption.

Factor Eight / Part III: The Patent Record

Worked examples versus claims

US11912707 and WO2022234524 describe preparative work relevant to 7-OH. Patent claims can cover proposed embodiments; distinguish those from worked examples and characterized products.

Commercial process disclosures

Conversion patent families and WO2025123039A1 identify possible industrial routes. Related patents in the same family should not be counted as independent experiments.

Limits of the record

A patent is not proof that a particular company manufactured a particular marketed lot by that method. The archive preserves these documents for examination rather than treating every claimed route as commercial fact.

Source distinction: patents, company statements, scientific findings and litigation allegations are not interchangeable. The proposed DEA findings have not been made by the agency.
