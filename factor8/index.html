Factor Eight

The case for examining mitragynine as an immediate chemical precursor of controlled kratom-derived opioids.

The question Congress anticipated

The Controlled Substances Act provides a specific pathway for immediate precursors. It addresses chemical intermediaries used or likely to be used in manufacturing controlled substances and permits preventive intervention. This archive tests whether mitragynine meets the statutory findings—not whether the agency has already reached them.

The evidence

The collection includes preparative chemistry, patents, commercial production accounts, scientific assessments, government records, litigation materials and laboratory reports. Each category answers a different question; no single document proves the entire statutory case.

The distinction

An immediate-precursor designation under 21 U.S.C. § 811(e) is different from an ordinary eight-factor scheduling determination under § 811(c). The three-part definition in § 802(23) must be addressed independently.
