Factor Eight.
The immediate precursor case.

A petition-led record for federal control of mitragynine: the law, the three required findings, and the original evidence.

Control the manufacturing input, not just the finished product.

This record supports a request to designate mitragynine as an immediate precursor and place it in Schedule I under the separate authority Congress provided in 21 U.S.C. § 811(e). The chemical and commercial evidence is organized around the three findings DEA must make—not around a pile of disconnected studies. [L1] [L2]

The proposed findings address mitragynine’s relationship to 7-hydroxymitragynine (7-OH) and mitragynine pseudoindoxyl (MP) separately. The petition seeks an affirmative agency determination on the documented routes, with 7-OH relief contingent on effective federal control of the relevant target substance.

The central legal point

Congress authorized control of an intermediary “used or likely to be used” and control necessary to “prevent, curtail, or limit” manufacture. The statute does not require waiting for widespread illicit production. It does require evidence for all three findings. [L1]

The evidence that carries the argument.

The strongest case comes from combining different kinds of records. A worked preparation identifies a chemical relationship. A manufacturer’s account supports actual commercial context. Product analysis tests composition. No single category substitutes for every statutory finding.

The target must actually be controlled.

The October 14, 2026 Federal Register notice is a notice of intent, not an effective replacement order. It states that the August 26 MP temporary order remains in force until it expires or is replaced and withdrawn. The replacement 7-OH/MP order is to be published on or after November 13; that is not an automatic effective date. This record does not treat proposed 7-OH control as completed scheduling. [L9]

The request therefore separates the existing MP target from a contingent 7-OH target, and asks DEA to apply the operative definition and scope of each target when making its decision.
