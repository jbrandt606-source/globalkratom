Part IV: Commercial Manufacturing

Shaman, Missouri litigation, and the question of the actual manufacturing input.

Factor Eight / Part IV: Commercial Manufacturing

Inside the facility

American Shaman describes a documentary showing a production process beginning with kratom leaf extract, oxidation, analysis and purification. The account provides commercial context but does not, by itself, identify the exact composition of every input batch.

The sworn record

Vince Sanders’ affidavit addresses the company’s products, infrastructure and records supplied to Missouri. The state’s complaints contain allegations, not adjudicated findings.

The missing production records

The affidavit describes more than 9,000 pages supplied to the Missouri Attorney General. Those records are not included in this collection. Their contents should not be presumed.

Source distinction: patents, company statements, scientific findings and litigation allegations are not interchangeable. The proposed DEA findings have not been made by the agency.
