Know what the search has searched.

A title match is labeled “Catalog record.” A PDF hit identifies its page and whether it came from extracted text, OCR, or a reviewed excerpt. Reviewed excerpts summarize a verified passage; they are not presented as full-page transcription. Check quotations in the original. An unmatched scanned page is not evidence that the subject is absent.

The package includes a ready-built index, so no GitHub Action is required to begin searching. The optional indexing workflow can add OCR coverage for image-only PDFs and rebuild after changes. Image-format COAs remain accessible through the catalog.
