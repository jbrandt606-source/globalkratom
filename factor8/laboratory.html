Part V: The Laboratory Record

Certificates of analysis, commercial product testing and the limits of chemical attribution.

Factor Eight / Part V: The Laboratory Record

What COAs show

The uploaded laboratory reports identify reported alkaloid compositions in particular samples. The source, analytical method, lot identity and authentication matter.

What COAs cannot show

Finding mitragynine and 7-OH together in a finished product does not prove one was converted into the other in that specific lot.

Independent testing

Commercial product studies provide evidence of high-7-OH products and related alkaloids. Distinguish analytical findings from inferences about manufacturing routes.

Source distinction: patents, company statements, scientific findings and litigation allegations are not interchangeable. The proposed DEA findings have not been made by the agency.
