Part VII: DEA Precedents

Immediate-precursor designations and the legal significance of prospective intervention.

Factor Eight / Part VII: DEA Precedents

ANPP and norfentanyl

Both are Schedule II immediate-precursor precedents involving fentanyl. They illustrate the legal mechanism, not a historical Schedule I decision based solely on Factor 8.

PCP precursors

1-phenylcyclohexylamine and PCC provide further Schedule II examples. A precedent must be compared on its statutory findings and chemical record, not merely its name.

What the precedents do not decide

A precedent involving fentanyl does not automatically establish that mitragynine meets the three statutory findings or that all botanical kratom preparations should be controlled.

Source distinction: patents, company statements, scientific findings and litigation allegations are not interchangeable. The proposed DEA findings have not been made by the agency.
