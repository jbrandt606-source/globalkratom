Search coverage and source standards

Last updated October 25, 2026. This update checks Pocatello and Natick legal hearing notices, Streator adoption reporting and Arlington ordinance notice. The October 22 nationwide sweep remains the broader baseline; not every local agenda was rechecked today.

  • Google News: feeds covered kratom hearings over 90 days, general ban/council activity over 14 days, public-hearing and next-meeting notices over 30 days, 7-OH council/rule activity over 30 days, and recent council activity over 3 days. Duplicate and syndicated headlines were removed during review.
  • Facebook and X: public posts provided or corroborated leads in Merrimack, Albuquerque, Seabrook, Oglesby and Monroe County. Posts were checked against meeting dates and underlying records where accessible.
  • Date standards: a routine council meeting, draft effective date, retailer deadline or article publication date does not establish a kratom hearing. Conditional reporting and planning calendars stay in the tentative section. Committee items are not labeled final votes.
  • Product scope: botanical-inclusive bans, concentrated/synthetic-product restrictions, age limits and retailer-specific restrictions are identified separately. Draft definitions are read together with the operative prohibitions.
  • Access limits: private groups, restricted accounts and unindexed posts were not available. Some official portals and full news stories were inaccessible, and some final agendas have not yet been posted. A search that finds no date is not proof that no meeting exists.
