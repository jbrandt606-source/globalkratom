Massachusetts · State House, Boston

Six-member conference committee reviews kratom regulatory language

Conference negotiations · activeHouse regulation proposal vs. Senate omission

The House version of the 2026 economic-development bill (H.5576) contains a proposed retail regulatory framework for kratom, including labeling, age, synthetic-alkaloid and 7-OH restrictions. The Senate amendment (S.3228) did not include those kratom provisions. The six conferees are negotiating differences between the two bills. This is not a newly noticed public hearing, and no date for a conference report or kratom vote was verified as of October 16, 2026.

Verification: the six House–Senate conferees are official. Conference negotiations are ongoing; there is no published date for a separate kratom public hearing.

Separately, Massachusetts emergency regulation 105 CMR 726.000 placed all forms of kratom in Schedule I effective August 28, 2026, for up to one year unless withdrawn earlier. An unadopted conference proposal does not alter that order.

Track: H.5576 official bill history · S.3228 Senate text

How to confirm a hearing

Check the official clerk or legislative agenda for the latest date, time, room, relevant docket number, opportunity for public comment, and any posted revisions. An ordinance can be introduced, heard, amended, tabled, or adopted in different meetings. This page does not count draft ordinances as enacted bans.

For bans that have already been enacted, see the U.S. Kratom Sales Ban Map. The map and this hearing page serve different purposes: one records adopted local restrictions; the other records future public proceedings.

Track the law, not the headline.

Read the posted agenda and the proposed ordinance before discussing a hearing or vote.
