Search coverage and source standards

Last checked October 21, 2026. The sweep searched Google News, publicly indexed Facebook and X posts, local reporting, state legislative schedules, municipal agendas, legal notices and proposed ordinance text. Every state and DC was included in state-specific or grouped searches. Government records were used to verify dates and scope when accessible.

  • Google News: seven news-feed searches covered kratom bans, hearings, council actions, ordinances and 7-OH, primarily over the prior 7–30 days, with a 90-day public-hearing search for longer-range notices.
  • Facebook and X: public posts supplied leads, including Streator, Clarion and Wilmington. A post date was not treated as a hearing date; pre-vote alerts and old or recirculated posts were checked against the underlying event.
  • Verification labels: official notices and packets, reproduced legal notices, news-reported dates and proposed scheduling motions are distinguished. A routine future council meeting does not prove kratom will be on its agenda.
  • Coverage limits: private groups, restricted posts and unindexed social content were not available. Some local agenda portals were inaccessible or had not posted final materials. This is a documented national sweep, not a guarantee that every local hearing has been located.
