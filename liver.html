Hepatotoxicity & Liver Injury

Published research documenting liver damage, jaundice, liver failure and transplantation associated with kratom use, explained in plain English.

Recent researchU.S. clinical studyReviewsPatient reportsSevere outcomesUnderstanding liver injury

Key Findings

  • Published research documents liver injury associated with kratom, including cases requiring transplantation.
  • Symptoms can develop within weeks and include yellow skin or eyes, severe itching, dark urine and abdominal pain.
  • Some patients have developed liver injury again after resuming kratom.
  • Reports include impaired bile flow, liver cell damage and combined liver and kidney injury.

Understanding liver injury

Jaundice
Yellowing of the skin or eyes as bilirubin builds up in the blood.
Cholestasis
Bile cannot flow normally out of the liver, potentially causing severe itching and jaundice.
ALT and AST
Blood test markers that can rise when liver cells are damaged.
Liver failure
Loss of essential liver functions, such as clearing waste and producing proteins needed for blood clotting.
Liver transplant
Surgery to replace a severely damaged liver with a donor liver.
