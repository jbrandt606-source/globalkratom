CDC poison center findings, 2015–2025

Suicide attempts accounted for approximately 25% of reports involving kratom and other substances, compared with 6% of kratom-only reports. Antidepressants were involved in 14% of multiple-substance reports. These percentages describe reported exposures, not all consumers. Read the March 2026 CDC report.
