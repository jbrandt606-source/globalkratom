NDI Denials

FDA New Dietary Ingredient (NDI) notifications for kratom have been repeatedly rejected due to lack of safety evidence. Multiple denials confirm kratom is not legally recognized as a dietary ingredient.

Official FDA Statement

FDA’s firm position: kratom is not lawful for use as a dietary supplement, poses serious risks of addiction, abuse, and death. Multiple public health advisories.
