Why this series?

This series explains how bulk kratom moves from Southeast Asia to the United States using public shipping records. It explains bills of lading, HTS codes, FDA Import Alert 54-15, and what public records can—and cannot—show.

Lawmakers, researchers, and the public need clear, factual information about the kratom supply chain. These articles provide that foundation using publicly available trade data.

Key concepts

Understanding the kratom supply chain starts with three foundational elements of international trade documentation.

Bill of Lading

The shipping industry's receipt showing who shipped cargo, who received it, and how it moved.

HTS Codes

Customs product classifications used to identify goods for trade and tariff purposes.

FDA Import Alert 54-15

Explains FDA's import policy for certain kratom-related products and why it matters.
