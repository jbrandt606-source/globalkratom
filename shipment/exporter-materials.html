Why Compare Independent Sources?

Bills of lading identify the company named as shipper. Public-facing materials show how that company—or sources describing it—presents the business outside the shipping record.

This review compares three companies selected because they combine substantial documented volume with either a meaningful public footprint or a distinctive classification pattern: CV Cahaya, ZYAM Trading 168, and PT Sahabat Sukses Kalimantan Barat.

Selection note: These are case studies, not a claim that all three are the three largest Indonesia-linked names by weight. ZYAM is a Thailand-based supplier included because its public profile and mixed HS-code pattern make it especially relevant to the classification investigation.
Evidence rule: A company website, LinkedIn profile, business directory, or government announcement does not prove the intended use of every shipment. The evidentiary value increases when the entity, product form, date, buyer, route, and shipment can be directly connected.

Methodology

Public web materials were reviewed as available on August 2, 2026. A source that was inaccessible, incomplete, or available only through a directory is described that way rather than treated as an official company statement.

Case Study 1: CV Cahaya / CGBA Kratom

CV Cahaya

Pontianak, West Kalimantan · Largest normalized supplier by documented weight in the workbook

Public marketing + government record
41 curated records 1,772.082 metric tons 7 importer relationships HS 1211.90 on all 41 records

What the shipping workbook says

The largest documented relationship was South Asia Trade: 25 curated records representing 1,102.331 metric tons. Other recurring consignee names included Deent Import, Far East Global, and Krown Logistics.

1211.90: 41No workbook disclaimer flags

What its public materials say

The company’s LinkedIn profile calls it a kratom-powder manufacturer and wholesale exporter. It advertises its own factory and warehouse, nano, micro, and mesh-30 powder, raw material for kratom extract, purity and potency testing, export compliance, and a stated production capacity of 135 tons per month.

The profile lists cgbakratom.com; the site was not accessible during this review.

Documentary comparison: The public marketing and workbook are broadly consistent in identifying a large-scale kratom-powder export business. The materials reviewed openly identify kratom and extract manufacturing inputs. They do not provide direct consumer serving or dosing instructions.

Government Description of the CV Cahaya Export

Indonesia's public information service reported that the Minister of Trade released a 351-metric-ton, 13-container CV Cahaya shipment valued at approximately US$1.05 million in February 2025. The government described the cargo as kratom powder that could be processed into health and herbal products and said PT Oneject provided sterilization or irradiation services before export.

This is a government description of the export program—not proof of how every U.S.-bound CV Cahaya shipment was marketed after arrival.

Case Study 2: ZYAM Trading 168

ZYAM Trading 168 Co., Ltd.

Bangkok, Thailand · Major supplier to South Asia Trade and Krown Logistics

Public profile + company registry

What the shipping workbook says

Fifteen curated records were linked to South Asia Trade and four to Krown Logistics. The HS mix was 1211.90 on 15 records, 3101.00 on three records, and both codes on one mixed-coded record.

1211.90: 153101.00: 3Mixed: 1

What its public materials say

Its LinkedIn profile describes the business as “Thailand's Leading Kratom Aggregator & Biomass Supplier” in wholesale import and export. A Thai corporate-directory profile lists the company as active, registered on February 17, 2025, with an address on Charoen Nakhon Road in Bangkok.

Documentary comparison: The public profile expressly identifies kratom and biomass supply. It does not explain why materially similar shipments appeared under botanical, fertilizer, and mixed-code records. No consumer serving or dosing instructions were located in the reviewed public materials.

Case Study 3: PT Sahabat Sukses Kalimantan Barat

PT Sahabat Sukses Kalimantan Barat

Kubu Raya, West Kalimantan · Limited public kratom-marketing footprint

Business directories + trade records
20 curated records 733.575 metric tons 6 importer relationships 1 fertilizer-coded house bill

What the shipping workbook says

Nineteen curated records used 1211.90. House bill KRPB2606201, arriving July 22, 2026, described 82.2 metric tons of Mitragyna speciosa under 3101.00 and named South Asia Trade as consignee.

Recurring U.S. relationships included Golden Mitra Botanicals, Usindo Exim Consulting, K Jo, South Asia Trade, Jap Empore, and Steven Tupuola Atamai Holdings.

What public-facing profiles say

Dun & Bradstreet categorizes the company under freight transportation arrangement. Go4WorldBusiness describes a distributor of palm-kernel shell and provider of shipping services, established in 2019. Those profiles do not prominently describe a kratom-product business.

Trade databases, by contrast, identify repeated exports of Mitragyna speciosa and the United States as the principal market.

Documentary comparison: The shipping record is substantially more revealing about kratom activity than the reviewed public-facing business profiles. No direct company-authored dosing, serving, beverage, capsule, or human-consumption statements were located in the public materials reviewed for this page.

What Counts as Evidence of Human Consumption?

None of the reviewed sources for these three profiles supplied direct dosing instructions or a suggested serving size. The evidence is more limited and must be labeled precisely.

Direct evidence

Serving directions, “take” or “consume” language, beverage preparation, capsule instructions, or retail-ready consumer-use directions.

Not located in reviewed sources

Commercial-use indication

CV Cahaya markets powder as raw material for kratom extract, and Indonesian government material described one export as powder that could be processed into health and herbal products.

Relevant context, not shipment-specific proof

General supply language

ZYAM calls itself a kratom biomass supplier. PT Sahabat's reviewed public profiles do not prominently identify kratom, despite the shipping history.

Does not resolve intended use

What This Review Does Not Conclude

This page does not conclude that any customs statement was false, that any exporter violated U.S. law, or that public marketing proves the intended use of every shipment.

It compares independent sources. Where the sources align, they reinforce the commercial picture. Where they differ, they identify the records lawmakers would need to request.
