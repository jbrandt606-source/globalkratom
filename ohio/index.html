What Are The Ohio Files?

In 2026, Krazy Daze and Botanic Tonics challenged the Ohio Board of Pharmacy over Ohio's treatment of kratom and mitragynine-related compounds. The resulting record contains far more than statutory argument. It includes a full hearing transcript, expert testimony, Botanic Tonics' own slide deck, the Board's exhibits, court orders, appellate filings and detailed exchanges with the judge about what modern commercial kratom actually is.

The Ohio Files focuses on the portions most useful to policymakers: what plaintiffs' witnesses acknowledged about opioid pharmacology and addiction risk, what the judge concluded after hearing the evidence, and why product-by-product retail regulation may be much harder than it sounds.

Evidence note: Testimony is attributed to the witness who gave it. Statements by counsel are advocacy, not evidence. Court findings are identified as court findings. The series does not treat every contested statement as established scientific fact.

How This Series Is Reported

The strongest claims in this series are deliberately tied to the record. Where a witness offered an opinion, it is identified as testimony. Where the judge made a finding, it is identified as the court's conclusion. Where a lawyer characterized the evidence, it is identified as advocacy.

The point is not to pretend all kratom products are chemically identical. The point is to show what the plaintiffs themselves placed into a public court record about opioid pharmacology, addiction risk, extraction, finished-product uncertainty and the scale of the retail market.
