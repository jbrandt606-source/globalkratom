THE REAL HARMS: EVIDENCE-BASED RISKS

Dependence & Withdrawal

Opioid-like dependence and severe withdrawal requiring medical treatment with buprenorphine or methadone.

LEARN MORE →

Cardiac Toxicity

Brugada syndrome, QT prolongation, arrhythmias, cardiac arrest, and sudden death in healthy individuals.

LEARN MORE →

Liver Injury

Cholestatic injury, liver failure, and transplant linked to kratom use. Recovery often after cessation.

LEARN MORE →

Pregnancy & Neonatal Risk

Neonatal withdrawal, limited safety data, and potential harm to developing infants.

LEARN MORE →

Youth & College Exposure

Rising use among adolescents and college students fueled by aggressive marketing and easy access.

Product Contamination

Heavy metals, microbes, pesticides, adulterants, and variable potency in many products.
