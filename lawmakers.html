Common Questions

Swipe horizontally to view the full table.

QuestionBackground
What is 7-OH?7-hydroxymitragynine is an opioid-active alkaloid present in small amounts in kratom leaf and also sold in concentrated products.
What is the difference between dependence and addiction?Physical dependence means the body adapts to a substance and withdrawal can occur when use stops. A substance use disorder involves a broader pattern of impaired control or harm.
What do poison center reports measure?Reported exposures and associated outcomes, rather than the total number of people using a product.
What is the difference between FDA and DEA authority?FDA administers federal food and drug laws. DEA administers federal controlled-substance scheduling and enforcement under the Controlled Substances Act.
How do state restrictions differ?State laws may address sales, possession, age limits, product composition or controlled-substance classification. Consult the current statute or agency record for the jurisdiction.
