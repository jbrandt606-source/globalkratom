FDA Authority & Federal Regulatory Status of Kratom

Federal law, dietary ingredient requirements, enforcement records and the distinction between retail availability and regulatory approval.

Retail availability is not regulatory approval

FDA has concluded that kratom is not lawfully marketed as a drug, dietary supplement or added ingredient in conventional food. That food and drug law determination is separate from controlled-substance scheduling.

Clinical research is not FDA approval

A single ascending dose study examines responses to increasing doses. A multiple ascending dose study examines repeated administration. These study designs can provide useful safety and pharmacology information, but do not by themselves establish safety for unrestricted retail use or effectiveness for a medical indication.

FDA funding, agency involvement or permission to conduct an investigational study must not be confused with approval of a drug application. FDA evaluates evidence for a specific proposed product and indication.

FDA explanation of drug approval and kratom · 21 U.S.C. § 355
